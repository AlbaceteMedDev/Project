# 🗽 First Time in the City — Host's Playbook (New York, fall 2026 edition)

**Built for:** a first-timer visiting Gabe, 3 full days on the ground (+ arrival evening), any age up to ~70 with normal walking stamina
**Shape:** 1 Midtown day · 1 Downtown + Brooklyn day · 1 Uptown-to-West-Side day · swap-in modules for a 4th day, kids, parents, or rain
**Facts checked:** Sep 12, 2026 (MTA fares, ticket prices, TKTS hours, Knicks schedule). Anything marked *est.* is a recalled figure, not a checked one.

> The plan makes the calls a first-timer can't (which deck, which ferry, which museum) so the visitor spends
> money and daylight once. Every day ends somewhere with food and a view, because that is what they will remember.

## ✅ DECISIONS ALREADY MADE (change only with a reason)

| Question | Call | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Which observation deck? | **Top of the Rock, booked for 40 min before sunset.** One deck only. | You see the Empire State Building *and* Central Park; the ESB deck can't show you the ESB. Two decks = ~$50 and 90 min wasted. Summit One Vanderbilt only if they want the mirror-room spectacle; Edge only if they want the outdoor ledge. |
| Statue of Liberty? | **Staten Island Ferry (free, 25 min each way).** Paid Liberty/Ellis ferry only if a Crown or Pedestal slot is booked. | The free boat passes within ~1 km of the statue with the skyline behind you. | Which big museum? | **The Met, capped at 2½ h.** MoMA is the Midtown add-on if they care about modern art; skip it otherwise. | First-timers burn out at 3 h. The Met is the one everyone regrets missing. |
| Broadway? | **Book one show 2–6 weeks ahead** for the evening of Day 3. TKTS is for a *second*, spontaneous night. | TKTS opens 11 AM Wed/Thu/Sat, 3 PM Mon/Tue/Fri, 11 AM Sun; the visitor is uptown at those hours on this plan. |
| Times Square? | **15 minutes, at night, on the way to somewhere else.** Never stay in a Times Square hotel. | It exists to be seen lit up, then left. |
| Brooklyn? | **Yes, on foot, over the bridge, ending at the Promenade at dusk.** | The best skyline view in the city is from Brooklyn, and it is free. |
| Airport → city | **JFK:** AirTrain ($8.50) + LIRR to Grand Central Madison/Penn, or a flat-fare yellow cab (~$70 + tolls + tip, *est.*). **LGA:** free Q70 bus → Jackson Hts–Roosevelt Av → E/F/M/R/7. **EWR:** AirTrain + NJ Transit to Penn ($16.80). | Uber from JFK at rush hour is routinely $100+; the flat cab fare is the hedge. |

## 🗓️ Day-by-Day — exact clock, meals & trains included

### Day 0 · Arrival evening
- **Land → city:** see the airport row above. Whatever the hour, get them tapped into OMNY at the first turnstile and explain "uptown vs downtown entrance" once, at a station, not in the abstract.
- **Drop bags, then out within 45 min** — jet lag or not, the first evening should be the postcard.
- **7:30 PM** first slice: **Joe's Pizza, 1435 Broadway at 40th** — folded, standing, $4-ish. Then 5 min north to **Times Square**. Fifteen minutes. Photo on the red steps. Leave.
- **8:15 PM** if they have energy: walk 42nd St east to **Grand Central** (concourse is open till 2 AM; the ceiling reads better at night). Otherwise 32nd St **Koreatown** for a real dinner that's open late (BCD Tofu House; Her Name is Han).
- **Early night.** Day 1 is the long one.

### Day 1 · Midtown — the postcard day + Top of the Rock at sunset
- **8:30 AM** breakfast bagel: **Ess-a-Bagel, 831 3rd Ave (51st)** — everything bagel, scallion cream cheese, lox if they're brave. Eat in the park at 51st.
- **9:15 AM** **Grand Central Terminal** — Main Concourse ceiling (backwards zodiac), the 4-faced opal clock, then downstairs: the **Whispering Gallery** outside the Oyster Bar (face the corner, whisper, the other person hears it diagonally across). 25 min.
- **9:45 AM** west on 42nd: **Chrysler Building lobby** (2 min, Art Deco), **New York Public Library** — Rose Main Reading Room, 3rd floor (free; opens 10 AM Mon–Sat, 1 PM Sun, *est.*), then **Bryant Park** behind it.
- **10:45 AM** north on **Fifth Avenue**: St. Patrick's Cathedral (free, 10 min) → **Rockefeller Center**: Channel Gardens, Prometheus, the rink (opens mid-Oct, *est.*), Atlas across the street.
- **11:45 AM – 1:45 PM** **MoMA, 11 W 53rd** ($28 online) — Floor 5 first: *Starry Night*, *Les Demoiselles d'Avignon*, Monet's *Water Lilies*, then down. **If they don't care about art: skip, and give the two hours to Central Park below.**
- **1:45 PM** lunch on the sidewalk: **The Halal Guys, SW corner of 53rd & 6th** (the original cart; chicken-and-rice, white sauce, a little red; ~$12). Sit on the plaza steps.
- **2:45 PM** **Central Park, south half** on foot: enter at 6th Ave & 59th → the Pond & Gapstow Bridge (the skyline-over-water photo) → Wollman Rink → the Mall (the elm allée) → **Bethesda Terrace & Fountain** → Bow Bridge → out at 72nd & Central Park West past Strawberry Fields. ~90 min, 2 miles.
- **4:30 PM** coffee and a sit at Columbus Circle; the visitor's feet need it.
- **Sunset − 40 min** (Sep 15 ≈ 6:20 PM · Oct 15 ≈ 5:35 PM · Nov 15 ≈ 4:00 PM — sunset ≈ 7:03 / 6:15 / 4:38, *est. ±5 min*) → **Top of the Rock, 30 Rockefeller Plaza** (entrance on 50th between 5th & 6th). Stay through blue hour; the city lights come on ~25 min after sunset. 75 min.
- **7:45 PM** dinner: **Keens Steakhouse, 72 W 36th** (mutton chop; the clay-pipe ceiling; book 2–4 weeks ahead). Budget version: **Koreatown** on 32nd, 10 min walk south, no reservation needed.
- **Late option:** rooftop drink — **Bar SixtyFive** at the Rainbow Room (65th floor of the same building; smart dress, no reservation for the bar, *est.*).

### Day 2 · Downtown + Brooklyn — memorial, harbor, bridge, dusk on the Promenade
- **8:30 AM** subway to **Fulton St** (2/3/4/5/A/C/J) — coffee/bagel at **Leo's Bagels, 3 Hanover Sq**, 6 min south, or grab-and-go at the Oculus.
- **9:00 AM** **9/11 Memorial** pools (free, outdoors, names cut into bronze; the Survivor Tree). **9:30 AM** museum slot ($36) — 2 h. If the visitor is a heavy feeler, do the pools only and give the museum's 2 h to the harbor.
- **11:30 AM** the **Oculus** (free — the white ribs; 10 min), then **One World Trade** from the plaza (skip its deck; you already have Top of the Rock).
- **11:45 AM** south on foot: **Trinity Church** → **Wall Street** (NYSE façade; Fearless Girl) → **Federal Hall** steps → **Stone Street** (cobbled, tables outside).
- **12:30 PM** lunch on Stone Street: **Adrienne's Pizzabar** (square pie) or **Fraunces Tavern** (Washington said goodbye to his officers upstairs, 1783). Skip the Charging Bull queue — a 30-second glance from across Bowling Green is enough.
- **1:30 PM** **Staten Island Ferry** from Whitehall Terminal (free, every 30 min, 25 min each way). **Right side (starboard) going out** for the statue; stand on the open rear deck for the skyline coming back. You must get off and re-board at St. George (5 min). Back at Whitehall ~2:45 PM.
  *Swap: if a Liberty/Ellis slot is booked, make it the 9 AM boat from Battery Park and move the 9/11 block to 1:30 PM.*
- **3:00 PM** walk north 15 min via Broadway past **City Hall** to the **Brooklyn Bridge** pedestrian entrance (Centre St & Chambers) — or 4/5/6 one stop from Bowling Green to Brooklyn Bridge–City Hall.
- **3:15 – 4:00 PM** **walk the Brooklyn Bridge** Manhattan → Brooklyn. 1.1 miles on the wooden promenade (bikes were moved to the road in 2021; no dodging). Best light on the towers is 3–5 PM in fall. Exit down the stairs at Washington St.
- **4:00 PM** **DUMBO**: the Manhattan Bridge framed on Washington St (the photo — 2 min, then move), **Jane's Carousel**, **Brooklyn Bridge Park Pier 1** lawns and Pebble Beach; ice cream at **OddFellows** (Water St).
- **5:15 PM** 10 min uphill to the **Brooklyn Heights Promenade** — the skyline at dusk, the reason for the whole day. Bring a layer; it's windy.
- **6:15 PM** dinner back down the hill: **Juliana's, 19 Old Fulton St** (Patsy Grimaldi's coal-oven pizza; walk-in only; arriving before 6:30 keeps the wait under 30 min) — or **Time Out Market** (55 Water St) if the group wants different things.
- **7:45 PM** home the scenic way: **NYC Ferry from DUMBO (Fulton Landing) to Wall St/Pier 11** — $4.50, the cheapest skyline cruise in the city; buy in the NYC Ferry app. If it's raining or late, the **A/C from High St** (2 stops to Fulton, 12 min).
- **Late option:** **Comedy Cellar, 117 MacDougal** (booked) or a drink on Stone Street.

### Day 3 · Uptown to the West Side — the Met, the High Line, the Village, Broadway
- **8:30 AM** breakfast on the Upper West Side: **Zabar's café, 2245 Broadway (80th)** — lox on a bagel; then **Levain Bakery, 351 Amsterdam (77th)** for the 6-oz cookie to carry into the park.
- **9:30 AM** cross **Central Park's north-middle** on foot from W 81st: Turtle Pond → **Belvedere Castle** (the little balcony over the pond) → the Obelisk → out at 82nd & Fifth. 40 min. (American Museum of Natural History is right there at the start — the kids swap.)
- **10:15 AM – 12:45 PM** **The Met, 1000 Fifth Ave** — a route, not a wander: **Temple of Dendur** (Egyptian, gallery 131) → **Arms & Armor** → the **American Wing courtyard** (Tiffany windows, coffee) → **European Paintings, 2nd floor** (Vermeer, Rembrandt, Bruegel's *Harvesters*) → **the Roof Garden** if open (seasonal; usually Apr–Oct, *est.*) for the skyline over the treeline. Hard stop at 2½ h.
- **12:45 PM** lunch: **Lexington Candy Shop, 1226 Lexington (83rd)** — 1925 luncheonette, egg cream, burger, the real thing; or E.A.T. on Madison.
- **2:00 PM** **6 train, 77th St → Grand Central**, then the **7 to 34 St–Hudson Yards** (its last stop). ~30 min door to door. (Cab ≈ 20 min, ~$25, *est.*, if feet are done.)
- **2:45 PM** **Hudson Yards** — glance at the Vessel (climbing is ticketed; not worth the time) → up onto the **High Line** at 30th St and walk it **south**, 1.45 miles, 60 min with stops: the 30th St spur, the 10th Ave amphitheater window over traffic, the sculptures. Down into **Chelsea Market (16th St)** — **Los Tacos No. 1** (adobada) as the 3:30 snack, Lobster Place if it's more.
- **4:15 PM** out at Gansevoort → **Little Island** (Pier 55; free; the tulip pillars; 20 min) → optional **Whitney Museum** ($30, *est.*) if the Met didn't fill them up.
- **5:00 PM** **West Village on foot**: Gansevoort → **Bleecker St** (Murray's Cheese; skip the Magnolia line) → **Grove & Bedford** (the *Friends* building, 90 Bedford — 1 min) → **Washington Square Park** (the arch, the chess tables, whoever is playing piano under it).
- **6:15 PM** dinner: **Via Carota, 51 Grove** (walk-in; at 5:30 or after 9:30 the wait is sane; before 7 it's an hour) · **L'Artusi, 228 W 10th** (book) · cheap and perfect: **Mamoun's Falafel, 119 MacDougal** ($5) or **John's of Bleecker, 278 Bleecker** (whole pies only; historically cash-only — carry $60).
- **7:25 PM** **1 train, Christopher St → 50th St**, 12 min → the **8 PM Broadway show** you booked weeks ago. (Doors 30 min before; most shows 7 or 8 PM; Wed/Sat matinees 2 PM.)
- **Late option (if no show):** jazz at the **Village Vanguard, 178 7th Ave S** (sets 8 & 10 PM) or **Smalls, 183 W 10th** — you're already there.

## 👨‍👩‍👧 ADJUST FOR

- **Parents 70+:** taxis between blocks (a cab across Midtown is $12–18, *est.*); skip the Brooklyn Bridge walk and take the ferry both ways instead (DUMBO ↔ Wall St); cut the Met to 90 min; Top of the Rock has elevators throughout; Staten Island Ferry is fully step-free.
- **Kids:** American Museum of Natural History replaces the Met; Central Park Zoo + Wollman Rink; the Intrepid (a real carrier, a real Concorde); Jane's Carousel in DUMBO; FAO Schwarz at Rock Center; the Roosevelt Island tram counts as a ride.
- **One day only:** Day 1 as written, but replace MoMA with the Staten Island Ferry (1 train from 50th to South Ferry, 25 min) and come back up for sunset at the Rock.
- **Five days:** add the Queens crawl and Williamsburg; give the Liberty/Ellis ferry its own morning; let one afternoon be unscheduled in the Village.
- **Rain:** Grand Central → the Met (or MoMA) → Chelsea Market → the Oculus → Koreatown. All indoors, all connected by subway.

## 🚇 GETTING AROUND — the cheat sheet the visitor should read on the plane

- **Fares:** $3 per subway/local bus ride; tap the same card/phone every time and after **12 taps in 7 rolling days** rides are free (**$35 cap**). Free bus↔subway transfer within 2 h. No MetroCard sales since Jan 1, 2026; cash on buses is ending this year.
- **Express vs local:** the map shows white dots (local only) and black dots (express stops). Wrong train = you sail past your stop; get off and cross the platform.
- **Entrances say "Uptown" or "Downtown."** Some entrances only serve one direction; read the sign before tapping in.
- **Distances:** 20 north-south blocks ≈ 1 mile ≈ 20 min on foot. Avenue blocks are ~3× longer. Streets run east-west and go up as you go north; avenues run north-south and go up as you go west.
- **Sidewalk rules:** walk on the right, stand right on escalators, **never stop at the top of subway stairs**, step out of the flow to look at your phone.
- **Taxis vs apps:** yellow cabs are metered and take cards; JFK↔Manhattan is a flat ~$70 + tolls + tip (*est.*). Ubers/Lyfts add a $1.50 congestion surcharge below 60th; cabs $0.75.
- **Driving in:** don't. $9 toll to enter Manhattan below 60th St (peak), $2.25 overnight, plus parking at $40–70/day (*est.*). Park outside the zone or at the airport.
- **NYC Ferry:** $4.50 per ride in the app; East River route = the best cheap skyline ride. **Staten Island Ferry:** free, forever.
- **Citi Bike:** day pass ~$25 (*est.*); the Hudson River Greenway and Central Park loop are the safe rides.
- **Tipping:** 18–20% at restaurants (20% is the default now), $1–2 per drink, 15–20% in cabs, $2–5 per bag for a bellhop. Counter service: tip screen is optional; 10% is fine.
- **Cash:** almost nowhere needs it. Carry $40 for carts, John's of Bleecker, and tips.

## 🧭 FIRST-TIMER RULES (what a New Yorker knows and a visitor doesn't)

1. **One deck, one big museum, one show, one bridge.** Doubling any of them is the most common regret.
2. **Eat standing up at least once a day:** a slice folded in half, a bacon-egg-and-cheese from a bodega, a halal-cart plate, a bagel with schmear. That is the cuisine.
3. **Reservations drop 14–30 days out at 9–10 AM on Resy/OpenTable.** Walk-ins work at 5:30 or after 9:30. Between 7 and 9 you will wait.
4. **Don't book the hop-on-hop-off bus, the Charging Bull line, the M&M store, or a "Statue of Liberty cruise" that doesn't stop at the island.** The Staten Island Ferry does the cruise for free.
5. **Layers, not a coat.** Subway platforms are 10°F warmer than the street; the Promenade and the ferry deck are 10°F colder.
6. **Wear the shoes you've already walked 8 miles in.** Day 2 is ~6 miles; Day 3 is ~7.
7. **Look up in Grand Central, the Chrysler lobby, the Oculus, the Woolworth lobby; look down on the High Line.** Buildings here are meant to be seen from inside.
8. **Do the postcard things without apology** — Times Square once, the bridge, the deck — and then spend the equal time somewhere the visitor didn't know existed (the Whispering Gallery, Stone Street, the Promenade, Roosevelt Ave).
9. **Sundays:** the Met opens late-ish (10 AM), NYPL reading room opens 1 PM, TKTS 11 AM, Chinatown dim sum is packed by noon — plan Sunday as the Brooklyn or Harlem day.
10. **The subway runs all night.** Late-night waits are 20 min; a cab home after midnight is money well spent.
