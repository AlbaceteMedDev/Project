Pricing Policy

Each skin substitute product distributed by Seller is priced at 40% of the applicable 100% reimbursement rate per unit of product, as set forth in the rate schedule below. The applicable per-unit rate is uniform across all sizes of a given product and applies to all Purchasers.

Payment Terms: Net sixty (60) days from Use Date, as defined in Section 3 of the Purchase Agreement.

Calculation — sheet products (per sq cm): Invoice Price = Length (cm) × Width (cm) × the applicable per-sq-cm rate. Custom sizes are priced at the same per-sq-cm rate as the corresponding product line.

Calculation — non-sheet / flowable products (per mg): Invoice Price = milligrams (mg) per unit × the applicable per-mg rate.
