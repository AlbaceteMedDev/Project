Pricing Policy

Each skin substitute product distributed by Seller is priced at 40% of the applicable 100% Medicare reimbursement rate per unit for the site-of-service MAC locality. Because the 100% reimbursement rate varies by MAC locality, per-unit prices and invoice totals are not fixed in this Schedule and are calculated at the time of order using the then-current locality reimbursement rate.

Invoice Price = 40% × (applicable MAC-locality 100% reimbursement rate per unit) × billable units

Unit basis: sheet products are billed per square centimeter (sq cm); Interfyl® (flowable / non-sheet) is billed per milligram (mg). Sheet billable units = Length (cm) × Width (cm); Interfyl billable units = milligrams (mg) per unit.

Payment Terms: Net sixty (60) days from Use Date, as defined in Section 3 of the Purchase Agreement. Custom sizes are priced on the same per-unit basis as the corresponding product line.
