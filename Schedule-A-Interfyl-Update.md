## Pricing Policy

Each skin substitute product distributed by Seller is priced at a fixed rate per square centimeter
(sq cm) by product line, as set forth in the rate schedule below. **The applicable rate is uniform
across all sizes of a given product and applies to all Purchasers.**

- **Payment Terms:** Net sixty (60) days from Use Date, as defined in Section 3 of the Purchase Agreement.
- **Calculation — sheet products:** Invoice Price = Length (cm) × Width (cm) × the applicable
  per-sq-cm rate for that product line. Custom sizes are priced at the same per-sq-cm rate as the
  corresponding product line.
- **Calculation — non-sheet (flowable) products (Interfyl®):** Invoice Price = milligrams (mg)
  per unit × the applicable per-mg rate. The per-mg rate is set at 40% of the applicable 100%
  reimbursement rate per unit of non-sheet skin substitute.

**Interfyl® pricing basis.** Interfyl is a non-sheet (flowable) skin substitute billed under HCPCS
Q4171 per 1 mg. The per-mg rate is set at **40% of the applicable 100% reimbursement rate per unit
of non-sheet skin substitute** ($127.14 + wage index per MAC locality; 40% × $127.14 = $50.856,
rounded to **$50.86 / mg**). Invoice Price = milligrams (mg) per unit × $50.86. Milligram content
per SKU is per the manufacturer's specifications; coverage (sq cm) is shown for reference only and
is not a pricing input. The national base rate is shown here; locality-specific rates apply per the
CMS MAC schedule and will be substituted per locality.

Prices are stated in U.S. dollars and are subject to change by Seller pursuant to Section 1 of the
Purchase Agreement. The per-sq-cm rate for each product line applies uniformly across all sizes of
that product, including custom sizes, and applies to all Purchasers.
