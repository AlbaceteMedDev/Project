Three things it words carefully

Places where the conventional phrasing does not hold up, and the kit says why.

Platelets

Not evidence of infection

The acute-phase platelet response is usually a rise. A note that starts antibiotics on a low count alone is hard to defend, so the count gets a differential and the antibiotic decision rests on clinical findings.

Consent

Real risks, not “none”

Low-risk and non-invasive, followed by the specific short list — irritation, silver sensitivity, contamination, non-adherence, and possible non-response.

Albumin

A marker, not a diagnosis

It falls with inflammation regardless of intake, so malnutrition is supported independently by weight trend, intake, and prealbumin.
