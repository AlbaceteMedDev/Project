This is a documentation aid, not clinical advice, and it does not make clinical decisions. Generated text is a draft: read every line, correct anything that does not match what you actually found and did, and sign only what is true. Benchmarks are the commonly cited four-week area-reduction thresholds (≈50% for diabetic foot ulcers, ≈40% for venous, ≈30–40% for pressure) — verify code selection, units, modifiers, and coverage criteria against your own payer policy.
