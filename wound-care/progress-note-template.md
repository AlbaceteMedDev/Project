### 8c. Application technique

Following debridement, the wound bed was [100]% beefy red granulation tissue. The
[Microlyte] sheet was **cut to fit** the wound bed and placed directly on the
granulating surface. It **adhered to the moisture of the wound bed on contact**,
so no additional saline hydration was required. The product was positioned to
**fill the wound bed with a slight overhang onto the intact periwound**, as the
manufacturer's IFU advises, so that the margin could be secured. **Steri-Strips**
were applied over the overhang to hold the product in place.

Per IFU, a **non-adherent contact layer (Adaptic)** was applied over the
[Microlyte] to protect it and prevent the secondary dressing from adhering to the
wound bed. An **ABD pad** was applied over that to manage drainage, and the area
was **wrapped with roll gauze** to secure the dressing stack.

Patient tolerated the application without difficulty.
